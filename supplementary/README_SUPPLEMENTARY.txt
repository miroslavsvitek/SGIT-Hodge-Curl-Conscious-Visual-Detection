Supplementary numerical objects for exact reconstruction of the frozen Hodge geometry used in SGIT manuscript v0.3.
S1: exact 64-channel order and nominal 3D montage coordinates used only for graph construction. S2: 222 oriented edges. S3: 216 oriented triangles.
S4/S5: exact selected 222x12 curl and gradient basis matrices. S6: mode ranks. S7: complete frozen NPZ.
These files are analysis-method supplements; they contain no participant EEG data.

The S1 coordinates are analysis/montage coordinates, not subject-specific digitized electrode positions or cortical source coordinates.
